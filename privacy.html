Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026. This policy covers the QuietCycle app for Android (package uk.jj.quietcycle) and this website. QuietCycle is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • Everything you put into QuietCycle is kept on your phone, encrypted. It is never uploaded, to the developer or to anyone else.
  • QuietCycle does not hold Android's internet permission. It cannot go online, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. The developer receives nothing from your phone.
  • Copies leave the app only when you make them: a report, a backup, a spreadsheet or a shared picture.

What stays on your phone

QuietCycle keeps everything in one file in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The file is encrypted with AES-256 using a key held by Android's Keystore, so a copy taken off the phone cannot be read.

  • The days you log: period days, flow, signs of a heavy period, pain, symptoms, mood, discharge, tests and notes, and, if you switch them on, sex and intimacy, water, weight, sleep and PMS diary ratings.
  • Your settings: what you use the app for, your usual cycle and period length if you gave them, reminder times and wording, the app's name and icon, and your app lock. The PIN itself is not stored: only a salted, one-way hash of it.

QuietCycle does not ask for the internet, your location, your contacts, your camera, your microphone, your photos or your phone's storage.

Health information

What you record about your periods, your body and, if you choose, sex and pregnancy is health information, and it is treated as such. It never leaves your phone unless you save or share a copy yourself. Nothing you type is checked, scanned or analysed anywhere but on the phone.

Reports, backups and exports

These are made only when you ask, and go only where you choose:

  • Report for a GP. A PDF of the period you choose. It lists what you logged. Estimates and fertile days are never included, and notes are left out unless you switch them on. It is not locked.
  • Backup file. Your diary and settings, encrypted with AES-256 using a key made from a password you choose. Your PIN is not in it. Without the password it cannot be opened, by anyone.
  • Spreadsheet. A CSV file of every logged day. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Share picture. A picture showing only what you tick: the day of your cycle, the next period estimate and a few words of your own. Fertile days and ovulation are never on it.

When you share a file, Android's share menu passes it to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy terms apply. QuietCycle keeps no record of who you sent anything to.

Reminders

Reminders are off until you switch them on. By default their wording gives nothing away. Android shows the app's real name, QuietCycle, on every notification, whatever name you have chosen for your home screen. Notifications are marked private, so a phone set to hide sensitive notifications shows only the discreet wording on its lock screen.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can change or clear any day, or delete everything at once in Settings, under Your data. Uninstalling QuietCycle deletes everything the app holds. Reports, backups and spreadsheets you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands: you can see it in the app, export it in full and delete it at any time.

Security

The data file is encrypted with a key that never leaves Android's Keystore. The app lock uses a PIN stored only as a PBKDF2 hash. Screenshots are blocked unless you allow them. Android's own app backup is switched off for QuietCycle, so your diary is not copied to a Google account without your knowledge. No method is perfect: keep your phone locked and your backup password private.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement. Downloads come from GitHub's servers on the same terms.

Children and young people

QuietCycle collects nothing from anyone, whatever their age. It has a setting for people whose periods have just started, which leaves out everything about sex, contraception and pregnancy. A parent or carer may want to set the app up together with a younger child.
