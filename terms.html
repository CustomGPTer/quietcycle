What QuietCycle is

QuietCycle is a diary for recording periods and how you feel, with general health information. It is not a medical device, a diagnosis or a treatment, and it does not give medical advice.

The guides are short summaries of published pages, mostly from the NHS, written for this app. Each names its source and the date that page was last reviewed. They are general. They are not tailored to your health, your history or any medicine you take, and guidance changes over time.

Estimates

The dates QuietCycle shows for your next period, for ovulation and for fertile days are estimates worked out from the dates you enter. They can be wrong, and they cannot confirm what your body has done. In pregnancy mode the week count is counted from your last period; a scan gives a more accurate date.

Not contraception, and not a fertility aid

Do not use QuietCycle to avoid pregnancy. Pregnancy is possible on days outside the estimated fertile days. Do not rely on it to plan a pregnancy either: it offers general NHS advice only and does not choose days for you.

Your health

If you are worried about your periods, your health or a pregnancy, speak to your GP, a pharmacist, your midwife or NHS 111. Call 999 in an emergency. Do not delay getting help, or stop or change any medicine, because of anything in the app. The "worth mentioning to a GP" cards may not appear every time they could help, and they may sometimes appear when they are not needed.

Reminders

Reminders may arrive a few minutes late, or not at all if Android holds them back. Do not rely on QuietCycle for anything time-critical.
