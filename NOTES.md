QuietCycle 1.0.0, the first release.

QuietCycle is a free, private period and cycle diary for Android. It has no internet permission, so it cannot go online. There is no account, no adverts and no tracking, and everything is encrypted on your phone.

- Today: a cycle ring showing the day of your cycle, your period days and what is coming, with one-tap period logging.
- Honest estimates: your next period as a likely date with a range and a plain label for how sure it is, worked out from your own last six cycles.
- Logging in a few taps: flow, signs of a heavy period, pain, body, mood, discharge, tests and a note.
- A calendar you can edit, cycle history, symptom patterns, trends and a PMS diary.
- Settings for hormonal contraception, periods that have just started, trying for a baby, pregnancy and perimenopause.
- 44 short guides, each naming the NHS page it comes from, and gentle "worth mentioning to a GP" cards.
- A PDF report for your GP, a backup file locked with your own password, and a spreadsheet of every logged day.
- Reminders that are off by default and discreetly worded, and a picture you can share yourself.
- An app lock, screenshots blocked, and a home-screen name and icon of your choice.

QuietCycle is a diary. It is not a medical device and not a method of contraception.

Android 8 or later. Website: https://customgpter.github.io/quietcycle/
