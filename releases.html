Release notes

What changed in each version of QuietCycle. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.0 22 November 2026Latest

  • First release. A period and cycle diary that has no internet permission, so it cannot go online. Everything is kept in one encrypted file on the phone, with no account.
  • Today: a cycle ring showing the day of your cycle, your period days and what is coming, with one-tap period logging.
  • Estimates as a likely date with a range and a plain label for how sure they are, worked out from the middle value of your last six cycles.
  • Logging: flow, the signs of a heavy period, pain, body, mood, discharge, tests and a note, saved as you tap. Optional sex and intimacy, water, weight and sleep.
  • Calendar: twelve months back and six ahead, with a mode for adding or correcting period days in one go.
  • Cycles: your history, symptom patterns, trends in cycle and period length, and a PMS diary that compares the week before a period with the week after.
  • Settings for hormonal contraception, periods that have just started, trying for a baby, pregnancy and perimenopause.
  • Gentle "worth mentioning to a GP" cards based on NHS guidance, each linked to a guide.
  • Learn: 44 short guides in eight groups, with search. Each names the NHS page it comes from and that page's review date.
  • A report for your GP as a PDF, a backup file locked with your own password, restore, and a spreadsheet of every logged day.
  • Reminders before a period and to log each day, off by default and discreetly worded, and a picture you can share yourself.
  • App lock with a PIN or fingerprint, screenshots blocked, six home-screen names and eight icons, light and dark themes, five accent colours and reduced motion.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.0 is called QuietCycle.apk. It is 2.4 MB (2,447,675 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

cbd24d7718840c1fc3c3e6236aced6f22d9a0ef35d856701e4dfed1165937b4d

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile QuietCycle.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have logged.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
