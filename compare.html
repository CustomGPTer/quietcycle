What QuietCycle does not do

QuietCycle is a diary. It does not confirm ovulation, it is not a method of contraception, and it has no temperature tracking, no link to wearables, no community and no experts to message. There is no partner app: you send an update yourself if you want to. It is for Android only and it is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site and install new versions over the old one.

Where the others do more

Flo has week-by-week pregnancy content and a partner mode. Clue can take readings from wearables and has pill reminders. Period Calendar can back up through a Google account and charts body temperature. All three are on Google Play and update themselves.

If you are worried about your health

No app is a substitute for a doctor. In the UK, speak to your GP or call NHS 111. Call 999 in an emergency.
